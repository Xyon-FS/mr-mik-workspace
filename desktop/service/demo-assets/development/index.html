A tiny architecture sketch

Pick up cup→Track warmth→Deliver / retry

Delivery controller

Owns the current delivery state. The art pipeline does not need to know its internals.

Coffee warmth

A separate timer emits progress. The HUD reads that value rather than calculating its own.

Decision log: why a separate timer?

Keep gameplay rules independent from presentation. A future UI redesign should not accidentally turn every espresso into iced coffee.
