The one-line pitch

A tiny midnight delivery game where the coffee must stay warm and the cap must stay impeccable.

Audience: people who enjoy short, cozy games and unnecessarily elaborate coffee logistics. This is an example campaign, not a market study.

A repeatable process idea
  1. Confirm the actual build and supported platforms.
  2. Collect approved screenshots and credits.
  3. Check links, captions and privacy.
  4. Publish only after explicit approval.

A reusable procedure can live in Processes. It is not a scheduled job and does not publish anything automatically.

Before going public

Review screenshots for paths, names and private material. Credit original work. Keep real credentials and transcripts out of Git. Then reward the team with exactly one more biscuit.
